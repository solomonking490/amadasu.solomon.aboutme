Research and publications

Power Beyond Oil: A Comparative Framework for Assessing Nigeria's Concentrated Solar Power Potential

2026 · Preprint, not peer reviewed
Featured
  • A research protocol with a preliminary diagnostic analysis: identical plant designs simulated in the System Advisor Model on harmonised weather data, with a pre-specified equivalence test.
  • Re-examines an earlier five-site comparison and finds that Sokoto performs as its direct normal irradiance predicts, a more defensible claim than a Nigerian advantage.
  • Shows that at a 12% cost of capital the capital charge alone (about USD 0.13/kWh) would exceed the 2024 global average CSP cost of USD 0.092/kWh.
  • Sets out staged pilots with decision gates and treats new converter concepts, including my own, as hypotheses to test.
