About me

I trained as an electrical and electronics engineer at the University of Benin and am completing a master's in Systems Engineering at the University of Lagos. At Galooli Advanced Solutions I validated remote-monitoring data from more than 150 solar, battery and diesel sites and maintained field equipment at telecom base stations.

Today I lead Covenant Energy Technology, developing clean-energy solutions for weak-grid and off-grid markets. I also facilitate seminars, workshops and climate-learning sessions, helping students and stakeholders understand energy transitions and practical climate solutions.
